# Human ICR Completion

The earlier 1,036-unit validation was withdrawn after 14 ineligible internal records were identified. A fresh validation sample was drawn exclusively from the corrected 980-note eligible corpus.

The corrected blinded human validation was completed by Milind Tiwari and Ausma Bernot on 26 July 2026 under Griffith University Human Ethics Protocol 2025/697. The frozen pre-adjudication dataset contains 1,032 paired case-target units across 313 evidence packets and 18 targets.

There were 981 exact agreements and 51 disagreements: 95.1% exact agreement, Cohen's kappa .839, and nominal Krippendorff's alpha .839. The binary Present/Absent subset contained 998 units, with 979 agreements (98.1%) and Cohen's kappa .933.

All 51 disagreements reached joint consensus: 22 Present and 29 Absent. Twelve cases received rationale refinements during Ausma Bernot's signed review; no final decisions changed. Reliability was not recalculated after adjudication. Controlled coder, evidence, rationale, signature, and record-level material is not published.
