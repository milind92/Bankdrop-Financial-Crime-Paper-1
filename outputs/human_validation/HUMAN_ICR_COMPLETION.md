# Human Inter-Coder Reliability Completion

## Status

The blinded human inter-coder reliability (ICR) and post-ICR adjudication stages were completed on 23 July 2026 under protocol `BD-HUMAN-ICR-20260722-R2`.

Ausma and Milind independently coded the same controlled evidence packets. They confirmed that their submitted answers were their own independent human judgements. Their original decisions were frozen before any disagreement review. No coordinator participated. After the ICR results were calculated, Ausma and Milind jointly reviewed all disagreements and reached consensus in every case.

## What This Establishes

The result documents strong consistency between two human coders applying the locked decision framework to the controlled validation set. It also documents complete post-ICR resolution of the 59 disagreements.

## What This Does Not Establish

The result does not:

- make the retained corpus a probability sample;
- estimate prevalence outside the controlled validation design;
- prove that an advertisement was genuine, delivered, purchased, or acted upon;
- establish offender, victim, transaction, or fund-flow counts;
- by itself report sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value, or negative predictive value for the deterministic rules;
- make optional LLM outputs human evidence; or
- replace author, ethics, governance, and target-journal approvals.

The 1,036 denominator is a count of paired case-target coding units, not screenshots, unique actors, unique advertisements, or the full corpus.
