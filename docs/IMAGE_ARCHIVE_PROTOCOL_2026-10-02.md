# Fixed screenshot archive: reproduction and author validation

This is the current bounded Paper 1 analysis. The historical 980-note mixed-record outputs remain in the repository for audit, but they are not substituted for the 1,037-image findings. Read the [Methods and Results report](IMAGE_ARCHIVE_ANALYSIS_2026-10-02.md) and the [aggregate tables](../outputs/image_archive_20261002/). The report is article-support text, not a completed manuscript or a declaration of claim validity.

## Defined frame and inputs

The archive contains 999 Markdown paths and 1,101 PNG paths. The historical screen selected 980 notes; 391 of these have at least one resolved local PNG reference. The 1,140 references include 1,048 resolved occurrences, seven missing links, and 85 external links. The resolved references address 1,043 local PNG paths; SHA-256 content deduplication leaves 1,037 unique screenshot contents. Thirty-five novel unreferenced image hashes are outside this linked-image frame. The unit is an archived screenshot content, not a unique post, listing, account, person, transaction, or time point.

The controlled script verifies each included PNG against its extracted copy, the original frozen ZIP member, and the OCR cache's SHA-256 value before coding. It applies the released Phase 3 regex patterns separately to each image's cached OCR string. A target is positive when one of its patterns matches at least once in that image. Markdown text and text from other images are not joined into the decision. The code has 12 typology families, six AML candidate families, and one quality flag. OCR extraction status was `ok` for all referenced PNG paths, but no gold-standard transcription study establishes OCR accuracy. All six images with fewer than 30 recorded OCR words remain in the primary denominator.

The portable [controlled reproducer](../code/image_archive/reproduce_controlled.py) requires authorised local inputs and a result directory **outside this public repository**. Its arguments are `--vault`, `--source-zip`, `--audit-dir`, `--legacy-icr-dir`, and `--output-dir`. The audit directory contains the replayed Phase 1-3 outputs and orphan-image audit. The script refuses to write controlled results inside this checkout. A verified rerun produced all 12 aggregate/controlled result files byte for byte identical to the original analysis. [Release provenance](../outputs/image_archive_20261002/release_provenance.json) records file-level input, code, and public-output hashes; it contains no record-level hashes or source text.

## What the complete computation supports

All 19 released OCR rules have been applied to the full 1,037-image frame. Public tables report positive counts, within-image co-occurrence, target overlap, pattern diagnostics, exact OCR-text deduplication, restriction to at least 30 OCR words, a unit-matched historical Markdown/OCR comparison, and leave-one-archive-group-out ranges without identifying archive groups. The percentages are descriptive proportions of this fixed archive, not sampling estimates for online markets. Closely related rules overlap: the jurisdiction and domestic-account-preference vectors are identical, and some generic pattern families support only lexical descriptions. No rule count establishes a real sale, cash-out, recruited person, or financial flow.

Collection instructions, actual screenshot capture times, source completeness, and creator attribution cannot be established from the supplied archive. Filename date tokens and ZIP modification dates are not treated as capture dates. This is a stated limit of the defined retrospective design; no missing history is inferred. Screenshot-level text, OCR, group labels, coder files, reasons, and image hashes remain controlled.

## Final five-construct author check

The five constructs selected for possible source-claim interpretation are bank-log offers/requests, bank-drop offers/requests, bundled identity/credential products, email or session access linked to account access, and bank-log access linked with email/cookie/recovery/session access. The focus was chosen after examining earlier mixed-record validation and the rule wording, so it is post hoc. The remaining 13 substantive rules are complete as exploratory OCR-signal counts; they are not advertised as validated source claims. The one quality flag is reported separately.

The controlled packet has a blank simple sheet for each author: 249 target judgments across 205 original screenshots per author. For each target, it samples up to 20 rule-positive images and 30 rule-negative images, selecting a census if fewer positives exist. Random sampling is fixed by seed `20261002`, stratified by OCR word count (<30 versus >=30) and whether an image basename appeared in a July packet. The private key records N_h, n_h, predictions, and image SHA-256 values; no such fields appear in the author sheets. Of the 249 assignments, 143 are mapped to prior packet exposure and 106 are not. This is an assignment count, not a distinct-image exposure count.

Each author independently reviews only the original image and chooses `present`, `absent`, `ambiguous`, `unreadable`, or `out_of_scope`. `present` means the image visibly makes the specified source claim; it does not mean the claim is true. The two frozen independent files allow overall and per-target exact five-category agreement and Cohen's kappa. A joint final decision is recorded for every disagreement and nonbinary judgment. Inverse-probability weights N_h/n_h then estimate rule validity within the fixed archive. Nonbinary outcomes are shown separately. The scorer also gives seeded, within-stratum bootstrap percentile intervals as descriptive uncertainty diagnostics. It does not create or report human ICR until real completed author sheets exist.

The July 2026 workbooks contain 1,032 paired judgments in 313 mixed Markdown/OCR packets. An applicability audit found 83 single-image judgments with no additional Markdown text and OCR exactly matching this cache. All 83 author decisions agreed, but 79 were `absent`, only four `present`, and they were spread over 18 targets. The old definitions and evidence presentation differ from the new screenshot task. The earlier kappa and rule-performance figures are therefore historical context and cannot be used as current image-level ICR or construct validity.
