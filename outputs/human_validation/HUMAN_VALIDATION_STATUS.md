# Human Validation Status: Withdrawn Pending Revalidation

The human-validation results dated 23 July 2026 are withdrawn from the Paper 1 evidence package.

A post-run eligibility audit found that the original sampling frame included 14 internal administrative or research documents. These documents contributed 59 paired case-target units to the completed coding dataset but are not part of the corrected 980-note evidence corpus. Because the sampling frame and deterministic rules have changed, the earlier agreement, kappa, alpha, Gwet AC1, and adjudication summaries must not be cited as validation of the corrected analysis.

A new sample must be drawn from the corrected 980-note corpus and independently coded before human-validation statistics are restored. Cohen's kappa remains undefined, rather than 1.0, when expected agreement equals 1 because of zero marginal variance. Binary Gwet AC1 is a labelled binary sensitivity statistic and does not represent the five-category decision set.
